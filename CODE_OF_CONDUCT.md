# Community Code of Conduct

We aim to keep VisionData Gate collaboration professional, evidence-based, and safe for industrial users.

Contributors are expected to:

- discuss technical claims with reproducible evidence and clear uncertainty;
- respect privacy, data licenses, safety boundaries, and human decision authority;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, or disclosure of confidential material;
- accept maintainers' decisions to remove unsafe data, unverifiable claims, or production-control shortcuts.

Report conduct concerns through GitHub's private reporting or platform abuse-reporting mechanisms. Do not publish personal or confidential details in an issue. Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and restrict participation when needed to protect people, data, or system safety.
